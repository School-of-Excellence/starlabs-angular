How it works
1 Mode 1 — File: Upload any audio file. DF3 processes it offline and gives you both RAW and Processed WAVs to download and compare.
2 Mode 2 — Live: Hear the filter in real time through headphones. Both channels (RAW + Processed) are recorded simultaneously — WAVs appear the moment you click Stop.
3 Mode 3 — A/B/C: Records Raw + DF3 + Koala simultaneously. All three are lossless WAV — the fairest side-by-side comparison possible.
4 A structured quality report (RAW · Processed · Comparison — same format as the call quality tracker) is generated after every mode. Download it as JSON.

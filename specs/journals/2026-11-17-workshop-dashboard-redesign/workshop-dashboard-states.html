01

Page states

Before any figure shows: loading, failure and a locked door. Each replaces the whole dashboard, exactly as today.

02

Workshop variants

Three switches change the top of the screen: evergreen, category-based (CP) and live. Total Enrolled always leads.

03

Challenge Progress Overview

One ledger row per challenge; open as many as you like. Counts never overlap and add up to the participant total.

04

Participant Progress Details

The working table. Search, sort, filter, export and select a person to read their data below.

05

Participant Data

The reading room for one person. Selecting a row fills it and scrolls here; ✕ clears it.

06

Submissions archives

All Assignments, All Forms and All VideoAsk, each behind its own right. Groups open in place.

07

Platform Usage

Always on the page. Legend rows and donut slices open "Enrolled via - {platform}".

08

Participant panel, every variant

One panel serves every clickable figure, status and platform. Header: title, subtitle, Email / WhatsApp / notification (communication right) and close. Then count + export, name search, filters, list.

09

Tablet and phone

Same content, re-flowed by the width of the content area (container queries), never cut.

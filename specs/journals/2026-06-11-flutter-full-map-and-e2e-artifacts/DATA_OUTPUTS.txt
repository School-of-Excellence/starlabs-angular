  profileid (str) | journeyref (ref->journey) | journeystatus (str: initiated/completed/upgraded/...) | journeytype (str:"new")

participantsproduct (sample 5) — the DELIVERY/ENROLLMENT unit. FULL field shape:
  profileid (str) | productref (ref->products) | packageref (ref->package) | status (str: cancelled/completed/shifted/null/ongoing/initiated)
  mode (str|null) | nextmode (null) | nextmodedate (null) | deliverymode (str: "Big Mode"/"Event Mode"/"Installation Event Mode")
  deliveryplanning (str:"normal") | deliverytype (str|null: "BiG Arena"/"Winning Heart") | minimumpayment (str|number)
  sequenceorder (number) | statusdate (object — a MODE-PROGRESSION TRAIL: {journeyplanningmode,initiated,ongoing,preparationmode,cancelled})
  subscriptionstart/end (ts) | unlimited (bool) | journeyref (null on all — product is journey-attributed via the PJP, not here)
  EVENT-LINK fields (present when the product is an event/queue delivery):
    eventref (ref->event collection OR ref->queue generation)  <-- can point at EITHER
    arenaeventid (str) | eventparticipationid (str) | queuevariationid (str) | instantiated (str:"done")
  NOTE the "shifted" status appears here too (one sample had status:"shifted").

journeyproductpurchase (sample 5) — per-purchase, carries the Watson cross-key + the SHIFT/UPGRADE label:
  profileid (str) | journeyref (ref->journey) | participantjourneyproductref (ref->participantjourneyproduct) | productref (array)
  purchasetype (str:"journey") | watsonpurchaseid (str, 100%) | watsonpurchaselabel (str)
  watsonpurchaselabel SAMPLES: "uP! Accelerate", "CTD to uP!", "FTM to BiG upgrade", "CPM upgrade to BiG", "Health Explorative"
  ^ the "<A> to <B>" / "... upgrade" wording is where a SHIFT/UPGRADE is literally spelled out.
